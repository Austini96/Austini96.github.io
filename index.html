About Me

I'm a Data Engineer at Ruan Transportation Management Systems, where I build production data infrastructure in Azure Synapse Analytics and Microsoft Fabric. I own the ETL/ELT pipeline for our Oracle-to-cloud migration, serve as technical lead for automated data lineage across 50+ pipelines, and model the fact and dimension tables analysts rely on for enterprise reporting.

My foundation is in data science. I earned an MS in Data Science from the University of Iowa, where I prepared and analyzed 20M+ patient records for NIH-funded healthcare research. That background shapes how I build: I care about data that is well modeled, validated, and traceable, so the people analyzing it can trust what they find.

When I'm not analyzing datasets, you'll find me looking for good restaurants, enjoying a good cup of coffee while listening to music, or playing songs myself with guitar or piano. I also enjoy experimenting with AI to build my own products, like a native macOS app for configuring Bose audio settings, and exploring new AI models and features as they come out.
